# Maplewood Family Clinic (fictional demo clinic)

## Opening hours
Monday to Friday: 8:30am to 12:30pm and 2:00pm to 6:00pm.
Saturday: 8:30am to 12:30pm.
Sunday and public holidays: closed.
Last registration is 30 minutes before closing.

## Location and parking
Address: 12 Maplewood Avenue, #01-05, Singapore 123456 (fictional).
Nearest MRT: Maplewood station, exit B, 3 minutes' walk.
Parking: basement car park, first hour free for clinic patients (validate your ticket at the counter).
The clinic is wheelchair accessible.

## Contact
Phone: 6123 4567 (during opening hours).
Email: care@maplewood-clinic.example (replies within 1 working day).
Do not email urgent medical concerns.

## Services
General consultations for adults and children, chronic disease care (diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol),
child health and vaccinations, women's health (pap smears, contraception advice), health screening packages,
minor procedures (wound dressing, stitches removal, ear wax removal), travel vaccinations, and telehealth follow-ups.

## Doctors
Dr Aisha Rahman: general practice and women's health.
Dr Daniel Tan: general practice and chronic disease care.
Dr Priya Nair: child health, vaccinations and travel medicine.

## Consultation fees
General consultation: $35 (before GST).
Chronic care review: $45 (before GST).
Telehealth consultation: $28 (before GST).
Medication, tests and procedures are charged separately.
CHAS and Pioneer/Merdeka Generation subsidies apply for eligible patients; bring your card.

## Payment and insurance
We accept cash, NETS, credit cards and PayNow.
Selected corporate insurance panels are accepted; check with the front desk with your insurance card before your visit.
Medisave can be used for approved chronic disease management and vaccinations.

## Booking, rescheduling and cancellation
Book through the patient portal or by phone. Walk-ins are welcome but booked patients are seen first.
Please arrive 10 minutes early. Reschedule or cancel at least 4 hours before your slot through the portal or by phone.
Bookings are only confirmed when you receive a confirmation SMS.

## Prescription refills
Long-term medication refills need a doctor's review at least every 3 months.
Request a refill through the portal at least 2 working days before you run out; collect at the clinic or choose delivery ($8).
Controlled medications cannot be refilled without a consultation.

## Lab and test results
Normal results are released in the portal within 3 working days.
If a result needs follow-up, the clinic will call you to arrange a review. Doctors do not discuss results by email.

## Vaccinations
Childhood vaccinations under the national schedule, influenza, pneumococcal, HPV, hepatitis A/B and travel vaccines.
Travel vaccines should be planned at least 4 weeks before departure. Book with Dr Priya Nair.

## Health screening
Basic screening (blood pressure, BMI, glucose, cholesterol): $60.
Comprehensive screening (adds kidney, liver, thyroid and full blood count): $150.
Fast for 8 to 10 hours before screening (plain water is fine).

## Telehealth
Video consultations are available for follow-ups, results reviews and medication refills, not for new or urgent symptoms.
Book a telehealth slot through the portal; the video link is sent by SMS 15 minutes before.

## What to bring
NRIC or passport, CHAS or subsidy card, insurance card, your current medications or a list of them, and previous reports.

## Medical certificates
Medical certificates (MCs) are only issued after a consultation with a doctor, in person or by telehealth.

## Emergencies
The clinic does not handle emergencies. For chest pain, breathing difficulty, stroke signs, severe bleeding,
loss of consciousness or other life-threatening symptoms, call 995 for an ambulance or go to the nearest A&E.
If you are having thoughts of suicide or self-harm, call SOS (Samaritans of Singapore) on 1767, available 24 hours.
